## Overview

A horizontally-scalable double-entry ledger with strict consistency guarantees,
designed to never lose or double-count a transaction.

## Highlights

- Idempotent writes keyed by client-supplied transaction IDs.
- Append-only event log with periodic balance snapshots.
- p99 write latency under 12ms at 10k transactions/second.
