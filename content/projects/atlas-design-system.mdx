## Overview

A component library built so that accessibility and visual consistency are the
default, not an afterthought.

## Highlights

- Token-driven theming with first-class light and dark modes.
- Every component keyboard-navigable and screen-reader tested.
- Documented in Storybook with usage and do/don't guidance.
